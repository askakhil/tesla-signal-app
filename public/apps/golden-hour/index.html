🧭 3D Car Orientation — Sun & Sunset

The car stays facing up. The world turns around it as you turn — the sun and the sunset direction move live, reactive to your car direction.

Heading source: manual slider until GPS or compass is enabled.

How it works: sun azimuth is calculated on-device from your location and time. Sunset direction is the sun azimuth at sunset today. Relative angle = sun azimuth − car heading. On iPhone, Enable compass asks for motion permission — that is the device compass, not GPS. While driving, GPS heading (your direction of travel) is more stable than the phone compass.

📸 Street-photo tip

Golden light is low, warm, and long-shadowed. It makes ordinary streets look like a film still — shoot fast, the light changes every few minutes.
