Projection = your pace so far (miles per day since lease start) carried forward to lease end. It is a simple pace forecast, not a lease quote — it does not include overage charges, fees, or a buyout.

Privacy: everything you enter stays in this browser's localStorage only. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere — including your mileage figures. Clear My Data removes it from this browser. If you clear browser data, this tracker resets. Works in a phone browser mounted in any car, and in a Tesla browser.
